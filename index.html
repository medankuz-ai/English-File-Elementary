Possessive adjectives: my, your, his, her, its, our, their

Grammar help / Подсказка

I → my · you → your · he → his · she → her · it → its · we → our · they → their

Use a possessive adjective before a noun: her name, their teacher. Choose it according to the owner, not the object.
